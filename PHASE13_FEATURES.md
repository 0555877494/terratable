## ✨ New Features Added in Phase 13

### 1. **Gift Finder Quiz** 🎁
**Route:** `/gift-finder`  
**Component:** `src/pages/GiftFinder.tsx`

**Features:**
- Interactive 5-step quiz to find the perfect gift
- Step 1: Who are you shopping for? (6 options: Mom, Dad, Partner, Friend, Colleague, Self)
- Step 2: What's the occasion? (6 options: Birthday, Anniversary, Holiday, Thank You, Just Because, Housewarming)
- Step 3: What's your budget? (4 ranges: Under $25, $25-$50, $50-$100, Over $100)
- Step 4: What flavors do they love? (6 options: Sweet, Savory, Exotic, Classic, Healthy, Indulgent)
- Step 5: Personalized product recommendations based on answers
- Smart filtering algorithm that matches products to preferences
- Add recommended products directly to cart
- Progress bar showing quiz completion
- Beautiful animations and transitions
- Option to start over or view cart

**Why it matters:**
- Helps users discover products they might not find otherwise
- Increases average order value through personalized recommendations
- Reduces decision fatigue for gift shoppers
- Creates an engaging, interactive experience
- Perfect for holiday seasons and special occasions

### 3. **Price Drop Alerts** 🔔
**Component:** `src/components/PriceDropAlerts.tsx`

**Features:**
- Automatically monitors wishlist items for price drops
- Floating notification panel (top-right corner)
- Shows items with price reductions
- Displays original price (strikethrough) and new price
- Shows discount percentage (e.g., -20%)
- Product images and names
- Direct link to wishlist
- Dismiss functionality
- Animated bell icon to draw attention
- Auto-shows after 3 seconds (if price drops detected)
- Smooth enter/exit animations
- Mobile-responsive design

**Why it matters:**
- Encourages purchases through urgency
- Rewards users for adding items to wishlist
- Increases conversion rates for wishlist items
- Creates excitement around deals
- Proactive customer engagement

### 4. **Product Variants Selector** 🎨
**Component:** `src/components/ProductVariants.tsx`

**Features:**
- Support for multiple variant types:
  - Size variants (S, M, L, XL)
  - Color variants (Red, Blue, Green, etc.)
  - Package variants (Single, Pack of 3, Gift Box)
- Visual selection with images (optional)
- Price display for each variant
- Stock status indicator
- Low stock warning (shows "Only X left")
- Out of stock handling (disabled with visual indicator)
- Selected variant highlighting with checkmark
- Responsive grid layout (3 columns)
- Smooth hover and tap animations
- Dark mode support
- Accessible design

**Why it matters:**
- Allows products to have multiple options
- Improves user experience with clear variant selection
- Reduces confusion about product options
- Handles inventory management per variant
- Professional e-commerce functionality
