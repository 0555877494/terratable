# 📖 User Manual - Terra & Table

Welcome to Terra & Table! This guide will help you navigate and use all features of our premium artisan food marketplace.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner
2. Choose your account type:
   - **Customer** - Shop and order products
   - **Delivery Agent** - Deliver orders and earn money
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Password (minimum 6 characters)
4. Click "Create Account"

### Logging In

1. Click "Sign In" in the top right corner
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

### Demo Accounts

## 🛍️ Shopping

### Browsing Products

**Home Page:**
- View featured products in the main grid
- Use category filters (Pantry, Beverages, Spices, etc.)
- Sort by: Featured, Price (Low/High), Rating, Name
- Use search bar to find specific products

**Search & Filter:**
- Type in the search bar for instant results
- Click category buttons to filter by type
- Use the "Advanced" button for detailed filters:
  - Price range slider
  - Minimum rating filter
  - Multiple category selection
  - Origin country filter

### Product Details

Click any product to see:
- Large product images with zoom
- Full description
- Price and any discounts
- Origin and weight information
- Customer reviews and ratings
- Stock availability
- Add to cart button
- Add to wishlist button

### Quick View

Hover over a product and click "Quick View" to:
- See product details without leaving the page
- Select quantity
- Add to cart directly
- View reviews

## 🛒 Shopping Cart

### Adding Items

1. Click "Add" button on any product
2. Or use "Quick View" and select quantity
3. Cart icon shows item count
4. Click cart icon to view your cart

### Managing Cart

**Cart Page:**
- View all items in your cart
- Adjust quantities with +/- buttons
- Remove items with trash icon
- See subtotal, tax, and total
- Apply coupon codes
- Save cart for later

**Saved Carts:**
- Click "Save Current Cart"
- Name your cart (e.g., "Holiday Shopping")
- Load saved carts anytime
- Delete saved carts when no longer needed

### Applying Coupons

1. Go to cart page
2. Find "Coupon Input" section
3. Enter your coupon code
4. Click "Apply"
5. Discount is automatically applied
6. See updated total

**Available Demo Coupons:**
- `WELCOME10` - 10% off for new customers
- `SAVE5` - $5 off orders over $25
- `GOLD20` - 20% off orders over $100
